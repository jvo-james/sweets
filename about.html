About Sweets

We like perfume
to feel easy.

Not too serious. Not too precious. Just good scents, good bottles and a reason to wear them.

Everyday fragranceMade to be wornAccra, Ghana

Sweets started with a simple idea: you should be able to pick a perfume by how you want to feel, not by a long list of words you have never heard before.

So we keep the names clear. The notes are simple. The bottles look good enough to leave on the dresser. And the shop should be easy to use.

Some days call for a clean citrus scent. Some call for vanilla and amber. Some call for something dark. There is no one right way to smell.

2Good bottles. Good memories.

The Sweets approach

From first spray
to dry down.

We build a collection around the moments people actually live: mornings that start slow, plans that run late, clean shirts, warm rooms and the little rituals in between.

The result is fragrance with a point of view, but without the pressure to know everything about perfume before you can choose one.
